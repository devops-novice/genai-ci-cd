# 📚 AI Leadership Reading Plan (July 2025 – March 2026)

This document outlines a curated, month-by-month reading plan for building strong foundations in AI-integrated engineering leadership. The reading roadmap blends technical depth, strategic thinking, responsible governance, and communication—all essential for professionals working at the intersection of DevOps, DevSecOps, OSPO, and platform engineering.

---

## 📆 Monthly Book Plan

| Month       | Book Title                                                | Author(s)                         | Focus Area                                                   |
|-------------|------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| July 2025   | *You Look Like a Thing and I Love You*                    | Janelle Shane                     | Quirky intro to GenAI limitations and behavior                |
| August 2025 | *Thinking in Systems*                                     | Donella Meadows                   | Systems thinking for platform design and feedback loops       |
| September 2025 | *Machine Learning Engineering*                         | Andriy Burkov                     | Real-world ML deployment and lifecycle orchestration          |
| October 2025 | *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* (Ch. 1–6)        | Martin Kleppmann                  | Foundation for scalable, reliable data and AI pipelines       |
| November 2025 | *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* (Ch. 7–end)     | Martin Kleppmann                  | Continued learning on fault tolerance, distributed design     |
| December 2025 | *Architecting AI Solutions for Risk Governance* (PDF)   | IBM                               | AI risk patterns, BFSI-aligned frameworks                     |
| January 2026 | *Ethics of AI and Big Data*                              | Peter Kirchschlaeger              | AI ethics, privacy, fairness, and responsible deployment      |
| February 2026 | *Competing in the Age of AI*                            | Marco Iansiti & Karim Lakhani     | Business transformation and platform strategy with AI         |
| March 2026  | *Never Split the Difference*                              | Chris Voss                        | High-stakes negotiation and communication                     |

--- 

## 📌 Notes

- Each book is tied to a specific skill cluster in the larger AI engineering journey.
- Readings are designed to complement hands-on work with LangChain, MLflow, RAG, and MLOps workflows.
- No proprietary or paid learning path required. Wherever possible, free resources and open publications are preferred.
